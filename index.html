Yogurt Project

Yogurt has been consumed for thousands of years, with origins traced to ancient civilizations such as the Egyptians, Greeks, and Romans, and references in Indian Ayurvedic texts dating back to 6000 BCE. The word "yogurt" comes from the Turkish word yoğurmak, meaning to thicken or curdle. Early nomadic herdsmen discovered that milk carried in animal skin pouches would naturally ferment due to body heat, producing a longer-lasting, edible product.
